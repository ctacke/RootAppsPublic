Need help with **GoWest**? You're in the right place.

GoWest is a text-based trail survival game for [Root](https://www.root.io/)
communities — lead a party of four and two oxen west along the Oregon,
California, Santa Fe, or Mormon Pioneer trail, managing food, water, health and
whatever the trail throws at you.

## Getting Help

To report a bug or request a feature, email
[ctacke@gmail.com](mailto:ctacke@gmail.com).

To help resolve issues quickly, please include:

- What you were trying to do, and the command you entered
- What happened instead
- The trail and difficulty you were playing, and roughly how far along you were

We aim to respond as soon as we reasonably can.

## Common Questions

**How do I start a game?**
Start a game with `start <trail>` — for example `start oregon` — optionally
followed by a difficulty: `easy`, `normal`, or `hard`. Normal is the default.

**Which trail should I pick first?**
Any of the four works, but the trails differ in length, terrain and how much
water you'll find along the way. If it's your first journey, `easy` difficulty
gives you more starting water, money and a larger water barrel capacity.

**Why does my party keep running out of water?**
Water is the tightest resource in the game. Your party drinks every day you take
an action, and not every region has a water source — deserts and high desert have
none. Use `water` to spend a day refilling where a source exists, and buy extra
water barrels at forts and trading posts to carry a bigger buffer across dry
stretches.

**Can I play with other people?**
GoWest is a single-player journey — each player has their own game and channel.
The one place your games meet is on the trail itself: a wagon abandoned by
another player can be found and salvaged.

**Can I have more than one game going?**
No — starting a new journey replaces your current one.
