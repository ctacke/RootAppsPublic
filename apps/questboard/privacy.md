**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

QuestBoard is a gaming event management app that runs inside the
[Root](https://www.root.io/) platform. It helps members of a Root community
organize gaming events, manage tables and game types, make reservations, and
coordinate supplies. This policy explains what information QuestBoard collects,
how it is used, and the choices you have.

QuestBoard runs within a Root community. Your account, sign-in, and identity are
provided by the Root platform, and Root's own
[privacy policy](https://www.root.io/) governs the platform itself. This policy
covers only the QuestBoard app.

## Information We Collect

**Identity from Root.** When you use QuestBoard, the app receives your Root user
identifier and your community membership details — including your display
name/nickname and the community roles that determine what you can do in the app
(for example, whether you can administer events). QuestBoard does not receive or
store your password.

**Profile information you provide.** If you choose to fill out your player
profile, QuestBoard stores the optional contact details you enter:

- Email address
- Phone number
- Preferred contact method

These fields are optional. You can add, change, or clear them at any time from
your profile in the app.

**Activity you create in the app.** To make the app work, QuestBoard stores the
gaming data you and other members create, such as:

- Events (including notes), tables, and game types
- Reservations and the characters associated with them
- "Please bring" requests and the commitments members make to fulfill them
- Event rewards ("loot")
- Images you upload for use in the app

## How We Use Information

We use this information only to operate QuestBoard's features, including to:

- Show events, tables, reservations, and related details to members of your
  community
- Let organizers manage events and let members reserve seats and coordinate
  supplies
- Determine what actions you are allowed to take, based on your community role
- Display member names next to events, reservations, and requests
- Send event-related notifications to your community's notification channel
  through Root

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## How Information Is Shared

QuestBoard is a shared, community-facing app. Information you create is visible
to other members of the same Root community as part of normal use — for example,
organizers and other players can see who is registered for an event and who has
offered to bring items.

Beyond your community, QuestBoard does not share your information with third
parties, except:

- Through the **Root platform**, which hosts the app and delivers notifications
- Where required by law

## Data Storage and Retention

QuestBoard stores its data within the Root platform environment for your
community. Information is retained for as long as it is needed to provide the
app's features — for example, event and reservation records remain until they
are deleted. Your profile contact details remain until you clear them or your
data is removed.

If you would like your profile information or activity removed, contact a
QuestBoard administrator in your community or email us at the address below.

## Children's Privacy

QuestBoard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information
through the app, please contact us so we can remove it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the
"Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes may be
communicated within the app or your community.
