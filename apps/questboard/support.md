Need help with **QuestBoard**? You're in the right place.

QuestBoard is a gaming event management app for [Root](https://www.root.io/)
communities — it helps you organize events, manage tables and game types, make
reservations, and coordinate what to bring.

## Getting Help

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, email:

**Chris Tacke** — [ctacke@gmail.com](mailto:ctacke@gmail.com)

To help us resolve issues quickly, please include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your community and (if relevant) the event or reservation involved

We aim to respond as soon as we reasonably can.

## Common Questions

**Who can create and manage events?**
Access depends on your role in the community. Administrators can manage all
events, tables, and game types; organizers can create and run events; and
members can view events and make reservations.

**How do I reserve a seat at an event?**
Open the event from the Events page and reserve a seat. Depending on the event,
you may also be able to add a character or offer to bring requested supplies.
