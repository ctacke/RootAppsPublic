Welcome to the **Zork Trilogy** support page and adventurer reference guide.

Zork Trilogy brings the legendary Infocom text adventure series to the [Root](https://www.root.io/) platform in an authentic green-screen CRT console.

---

## The Trilogy

1. **Zork I: The Great Underground Empire**  
   Explore the ruins of the Great Underground Empire, gather the 20 Treasures of Zork in the trophy case of the White House, and navigate treacherous mazes and darkness.
2. **Zork II: The Wizard of Frobozz**  
   Venture deeper into the subterranean realm, facing the capricious enchantments of the eccentric Wizard of Frobozz.
3. **Zork III: The Dungeon Master**  
   The final and most atmospheric chapter. Solve mystical puzzles of time and shadow, and prove your wisdom to the mysterious Dungeon Master.

## Playing the Game

### Basic Commands
- **Movement:** `NORTH` (`N`), `SOUTH` (`S`), `EAST` (`E`), `WEST` (`W`), `NE`, `NW`, `SE`, `SW`, `UP` (`U`), `DOWN` (`D`), `ENTER`, `EXIT`
- **Inspection:** `LOOK` (`L`), `EXAMINE <item>`, `INVENTORY` (`I`), `READ <item>`
- **Object Manipulation:** `TAKE <item>`, `DROP <item>`, `OPEN <box>`, `CLOSE <box>`, `PUT <item> IN <box>`, `TURN ON LANTERN`, `LIGHT MATCH`, `ATTACK <monster> WITH <weapon>`
- **Game Management:** `SAVE`, `RESTORE`, `SCORE`, `DIAGNOSE`, `WAIT` (`Z`), `MENU`, `QUIT`

### Command History & Navigation
- Press **Up Arrow** and **Down Arrow** to cycle through previously typed commands.
- Press **Escape** or click **MENU** in the top status line to return to the trilogy game selector anytime.
- Use the **SAVES** button to create named checkpoints or restore previous saves.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my game progress saved automatically?**  
Yes! Every single move you make is automatically saved to your Root account. If you exit or switch games, selecting **▶ RESUME** will return you to your exact location and inventory.

**Can I create manual save points before dangerous areas?**  
Yes. Click **SAVES** in the top bar (or type `save`) to open the Save Slot Manager. You can name your save slot (e.g., *"Before Troll Bridge"*) and restore it at any time.

**How do I toggle the CRT scanline effect?**  
Click the **CRT:ON / CRT:OFF** button in the top right of the terminal header to toggle the scanline overlay.

**What is a Grue?**  
A sinister subterranean predator that lurks in pitch-black darkness. Always carry a lighted brass lantern or torch when wandering deep underground!
